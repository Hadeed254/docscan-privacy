Privacy Policy

Application: DocScan PDF
Effective Date: September 29, 2026

Summary: DocScan PDF is an offline, local tool. All your scanned documents, pictures, and recognized text stay strictly inside your device's local storage. We do not operate external servers and do not upload or collect your files.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how DocScan PDF ("we", "our", or "the app") handles user information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have complete ownership and control over your scanned files, documents, and data.

2. Completely Offline & Local Data Processing

DocScan PDF is designed and built from the ground up as a standalone, local utility:

  • No Cloud Uploads: The application does not upload, transmit, sync, or backup your scanned pages, images, or PDF files to any remote server or third-party cloud service.
  • Local Device Storage Only: All documents created, scanned, cropped, edited, or extracted with the app are stored exclusively in your device's private application storage sandbox.
  • On-Device Optical Character Recognition (OCR): Text recognition is performed entirely on your device using on-device machine learning models (Google ML Kit). The text and images processed during OCR are never sent across the internet.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

DocScan PDF requests only the minimum permissions necessary to deliver document scanning and file management functionalities:

  • Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Used solely when you choose to take photos of physical documents or papers to scan and convert them to PDF. The camera stream is processed in real time on your device and is never broadcast or remotely.
  • Storage / Media Access: Used exclusively when you explicitly choose to import photos from your device's gallery or export/download generated PDF files to your device's local Downloads folder.
  • Internet Permission (android.permission.INTERNET & android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used solely by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to retrieve and display advertisements. No personal files or document contents are ever transmitted over the network.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing maintenance and development, DocScan PDF uses Google AdMob to display advertising. The app is configured to request and render non-personalized ads.

Although the app itself collects no personal data, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-identifying technical data in accordance with Google's policies, such as:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or vendor IDs)
  • IP address (used to infer coarse geographic region for ad serving)
  • Diagnostic and performance data (such as ad impressions, click events, and crash logs)

For more details on how Google processes ad data and how you can manage your advertising choices, please visit:

  • Google Privacy & Terms
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

5. User Control & Data Retention

Because all data resides strictly on your local device:

  • Document Deletion: Whenever you delete a page or a PDF file within the app, it is immediately and permanently removed from your device's storage.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling DocScan PDF or clearing the application's data via your Android system settings completely purges all app data and stored documents from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

DocScan PDF does not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). The app operates locally and does not collect personally identifiable information from any user.

7. Security

Your documents are protected by Android's internal application sandbox security model. Other applications cannot access DocScan PDF's private storage unless you explicitly choose to share an exported file through Android's system share sheet.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
